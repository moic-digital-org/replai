## Voice file template

Copy this template. The script rewrites the first line (version and date). Keep every section short:
the whole file goes into every batch and must fit in 5000 characters.

    # Voice · <name> · <network> · version N · calibrated on YYYY-MM-DD

    ## Who speaks
    Title and company as on the profile; who they speak for.

    ## Topics of authority
    3 to 6 topics the person truly owns.

    ## Avoid
    Topics and situations the account does not comment on.

    ## Tone
    | Setting | This account |
    |---|---|
    | Warmth | (warmer, default or drier: pick one) |
    | Humor | (none or light) |
    | Emoji | (never or occasional) |
    | Lowercase and fragments | (no or sometimes) |
    | Slang | (no or light) |
    | Public disagreement | (avoids or civil and reasoned) |

    ## Form
    Typical length, how they usually open, how they usually close.

    ## Uses / Never uses
    - Uses: words and habits that are theirs
    - Never uses: words and habits that are not

    ## Learned rules
    At most 10, one per line, with the date.

    ## Approved examples
    At most 8 approved comments. A tone ruler, never a template.
